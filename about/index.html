What Is Becoming Faithful?

Becoming Faithful is a growing ministry founded on Jesus Christ. We are learning to love God and love people, ask honest questions, welcome correction, and grow together in love, hope, faith, wisdom, and everyday life.

It began through everyday conversations, listening to people’s stories, and taking steps of faith. The complete account—including the beginning of this website—remains available in my own words.
