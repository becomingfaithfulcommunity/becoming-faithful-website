Songs

Songs written by Joshua Marcotte

Am I the Wolf?

A sermon about Jonah got me thinking about the difference between what people see at church and what God sees in my heart. This song is my prayer for God to change the man nobody sees.

The Warrior in the Garden

I pictured a warrior who wants to rush to the front lines, but his Commander asks him to stay in the garden. I wrote this about how much discipline it can take to stay where God has asked you to be.

My Amazing Sister

I wrote this for my sister, someone I love. I wanted her to have a song she could come back to on a hard day and hear her brother say, in his own way, “I’m here with you.”
