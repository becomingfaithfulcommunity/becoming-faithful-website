Support Becoming Faithful

One Person at a Time

Becoming Faithful is built one person at a time.

Support helps create more time and opportunity to connect with people, listen to their stories, keep learning, create meaningful content, and reach people who may not have found Becoming Faithful otherwise.

Even without financial support, Becoming Faithful will continue.

Why US$1 a Month?

One U.S. dollar by itself is not enough to sustain the work of Becoming Faithful, but one person choosing to support with US$1 per month still matters.

The idea is not to put the weight of Becoming Faithful on any one person or ask anyone to carry more than they reasonably can. US$1 is intentionally small so that supporting does not have to break anyone's back financially by itself.

But when many individual people each choose to put something small into the same work, those small contributions can come together and become something much bigger and stronger than what any one person could create alone.

Together, that support can create more time and opportunity to connect with people, listen to their stories, keep learning, create meaningful content, strengthen the community that is already here, and reach people who Becoming Faithful has not yet had the opportunity to connect with.

Support Does Not Buy Influence

If someone chooses to give more than US$1 per month, that support is appreciated, but giving more does not give anyone more influence over the message, direction, priorities, or whose stories are shared.

Becoming Faithful should never become something where the amount someone gives determines how much their voice matters. The goal is to remain focused on loving God, loving people, learning openly, and serving one person at a time.

Becoming Faithful Will Continue

Financial support can help create more time, improve resources, and expand the opportunities to reach and connect with people, but Becoming Faithful does not depend on financial support in order to continue.

If no one chooses to give, I will still keep moving forward, keep learning, keep creating, and keep doing what I can one step at a time.

Support affects the time, speed, and reach of the work. It does not determine whether the mission continues.
