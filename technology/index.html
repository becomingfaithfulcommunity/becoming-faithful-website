What is email?

An email is a message you send over the internet. Your email account is like a mailbox. Its address tells people where to send you a message. In ana@example.com, “ana” names the mailbox, and the part after the @ sign names the service that receives its mail.

When you tap Send, your email service delivers the message to the other person’s mailbox. They can open it later. You can both use different apps and still write to each other.

Try sending yourself a message

  1. Open your email app, such as Gmail or Mail. If it asks you to sign in, use the email account you want to practice with.
  2. Tap Compose, New message, or the pencil icon.
  3. In To, type your own email address. In Subject, type “Practice.” The subject is the short title you’ll see in the inbox.
  4. Tap the large space below the subject and write “This is my first practice email.” Check the address, then tap Send (often an arrow).
  5. Open Inbox, where new messages arrive. Tap “Practice” to read it. To answer a message, tap Reply, type your answer, and tap Send.

Can’t find it? Look in Sent to check which address you used. If it’s in Drafts, it hasn’t been sent yet. If the address is correct, check your internet connection and look in Spam or Junk.

Take a photo

  1. Find the Camera app on your phone. Tap it once.
  2. Point the camera at what you want to photograph.
  3. Tap the large round button once.
  4. Tap the small preview picture to see your photo.

Send a text message

  1. Open Messages.
  2. Tap the pencil or Start chat.
  3. Type a contact’s name and tap the right person.
  4. Tap the message box and type your words.
  5. Check the name and tap the send arrow.
